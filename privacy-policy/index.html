Privacy Policy.

Information practices and contact details.

Effective: October 2026
Updated from the July 1, 2014 policy.

This policy explains how we handle information, why we use it, and how to contact us about your records.

1. The Information We Collect

Browsing & Technical Information

This website is served by our hosting provider. The website code does not collect or store visitors' IP addresses and does not include analytics or advertising trackers. Our host logs and stores visitors' IP addresses for security purposes, when they visit the website. IP addresses can be personal information.

The website code does not set cookies or use browser storage. The Contact page embeds Google Maps. Loading or using the map connects your browser to Google, whose privacy policy applies to that service. Linked services have their own information practices. If tracking or live application services are added, this policy will be updated to describe them.

Information You Provide

You may choose to provide information when contacting us, applying for employment or credit, or requesting a service. The information requested depends on the task. Please provide only what is needed, and use the delivery method specified for that service.

2. How We Use Information

We use information to get the job done: respond to your questions, fulfill orders or service requests, process employment or credit applications, and provide updates you request. We may also use information to administer our business, protect our services, and meet legal obligations.

We do not sell, rent, or lease your personal information. Service providers may process information on our behalf to support business operations. Information may also be disclosed when required by law or to protect legal rights. These operational disclosures are different from selling information.

3. Your California Privacy Rights

Where the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), as amended by the California Privacy Rights Act (CPRA), applies, California residents may have the following rights, subject to applicable exceptions:

  • Know: Request information about the personal information collected, used, and disclosed about you.
  • Delete: Request deletion of personal information, subject to legal and other permitted exceptions.
  • Correct: Request correction of inaccurate personal information.
  • Opt Out: Opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information, where applicable.
  • Limit: Limit certain uses and disclosures of sensitive personal information, where applicable.
  • Non-Discrimination: Exercise applicable privacy rights without unlawful discrimination.

Contact us using the details below to make a request. We may take reasonable steps to verify your identity before providing records or making changes. We respond within applicable legal timeframes; some requests may be subject to exceptions or permitted extensions.

For more information, visit the California Attorney General’s privacy guidance or the California Privacy Protection Agency.

4. Security & Responsible Handling

We take reasonable steps to protect personal information. No website, communication method, or storage system can guarantee absolute security. Please do not email sensitive identification or payment information unless we have confirmed an appropriate secure method.

5. Children & Outside Websites

Our website is intended for business and employment-related use and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided such information, contact us so we can investigate and remove it as appropriate.

Supplier websites, training platforms, maps, and other linked services have their own privacy practices. Review their policies before providing information.

6. This Website Review

Our host’s practices are described in its hosting documentation and its privacy statement.

This website does not provide an online application or collect application data. Contact our office for current application instructions. Phone and email links use your own applications.

Messages you send through phone and email services are handled through those services and our business communications systems.

7. Changes To This Policy

We may update this policy as our practices or applicable requirements change. The revised policy and effective date will be posted here. This notice does not limit any rights available under applicable law.

8. Contact Rinehart

Questions about privacy, access to records, or corrections? Contact us directly. To protect your information, we may verify your identity before releasing records or making changes.
