About This Website Review

This notice describes the staged redesign you are viewing. Updated October 12, 2026. It does not establish a new effective date for Rinehart’s company privacy policy, which is dated July 1, 2014 on the source website.

Read Rinehart’s Published Company Policy ↗

Privacy deserves clear answers. Here is what this review website does with information, what happens when you contact Rinehart, and where to learn about your California privacy rights.

1. Information On This Review Site

Browsing & Hosting

This review is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical information, including IP addresses and request information, to operate and protect its services. IP addresses and similar identifiers can be personal information; they should not be described as anonymous simply because they do not include a name.

The review site’s own code does not add analytics trackers, advertising trackers, cookies, or browser storage for tracking visitors. Hosting-provider practices are described in GitHub’s privacy statement.

Driver Application Preview

The driver form is a demonstration. Information you type stays in the page’s working memory while it is open. The form does not submit applications to Rinehart or save them in browser storage. Closing or reloading the page clears the preview. Do not enter sensitive information into this demonstration.

2. Information You Choose To Send

Selecting an email or phone link opens your email or calling application. If you send a message or place a call, you provide information directly through that service. The review site does not receive or store the contents of those communications.

Credit applications, customer tools, and official application instructions may take you to Rinehart’s current website or another provider. Review the information practices of the service you use before supplying personal details.

3. Rinehart’s Company Policy

The published 2014 policy describes server logs, possible cookies, voluntarily supplied information, service fulfillment, and requests to access or correct information. It states that Rinehart does not sell, rent, or lease information to outside parties without express consent. That wording is different from an unconditional promise that information is never sold or shared.

This staged notice does not verify Rinehart’s current company-wide collection, retention, sharing, security, or request-handling practices. A replacement company policy should reflect those practices before it receives a new effective date.

4. California Privacy Rights

The CCPA, as amended by the CPRA, provides rights for California residents when dealing with covered businesses. Depending on the circumstances, these include knowing about collected information, requesting deletion or correction, opting out of sale or sharing, limiting certain uses of sensitive information, and protection against discrimination for exercising those rights.

Coverage, verification requirements, and exceptions matter. Operating in California alone does not establish that a business is subject to the CCPA. This review does not determine Rinehart’s coverage or promise immediate deletion or correction. Learn more from the California Attorney General and the California Privacy Protection Agency.

5. Children & Outside Websites

This review is intended for business and employment-related information, not children. Its driver form does not collect submissions. Rinehart’s published company policy addresses children under 13. Links to supplier sites, training platforms, maps, and other websites lead to services with their own privacy practices.
