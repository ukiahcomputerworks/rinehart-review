    Driver Positions Available:

    ♦ 2 positions located in Ukiah, CA
    ♦ 2 positions located in Benicia, CA

    Rinehart Oil is looking for truck drivers who meet the following requirements:

    ♦ Must be 21 years old
    ♦ Have a CDL with tanker and hazardous endorsements
    ♦ One year Class A driver experience
    ♦ Have availability to get a TWIC Card and TSA clearance
    ♦ Clean DMV report, 0 points in past 12 months or 1.5 points in past 3 years
    ♦ Must pass a DOT drug test
    ♦ Must pass a road test
    ♦ Must be able to lift 60 - 80 lbs.
    ♦ Willing to work nights, weekends and holidays on a rotating schedule
    ♦ Work well with others.

    The company offers full benefits which include Medical, Dental, Vision, Life, 401K, Holiday Pay, Sick Pay and Vacation. Also, no overnight shifts. We will train drivers not previously in Tanker or Petroleum Industry.

    For more information, contact the Office of Rinehart Oil at (707) 462-8811.
