Driver Qualifications & Application PDF

Confirm current requirements with the office. The existing driver qualification checklist includes:

  • At least 21 years old.
  • A Class A CDL with tanker and hazardous materials endorsements.
  • At least one year of Class A driving experience.
  • Availability to obtain a TWIC card and TSA clearance.
  • A clean DMV report: 0 points in the past 12 months, or no more than 1.5 points in the past 3 years.
  • Pass a DOT drug test and a road test.
  • Perform lifting duties involving 60–80 lbs., with or without reasonable accommodation.
  • Willingness to work nights, weekends and holidays on a rotating schedule.
  • Work cooperatively with others.
